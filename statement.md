# Problem Statement & Scope

## Problem Statement
Individuals frequently struggle to manage chronic medication schedules, track daily health vitals, and maintain accurate medical history records. Manual tracking often results in missed doses, adverse drug interactions, and lost clinical records. A structured, automated system is needed to consolidate patient health records, monitor dosage adherence, and provide timely reminders.

## Scope of the Project
This project provides a command-line Java application integrated with a relational database via JDBC. It enables users to record personal profiles, manage prescriptions, log health metrics, receive multithreaded scheduled reminders, and export adherence records.

## Target Users
- Outpatients managing recurring or long-term medication regimens.
- Caregivers tracking treatment compliance for family members.
- Individuals seeking a centralized system to organize medical visit history and health records.

## High-Level Features
- Patient profile creation and management
- Prescription tracking with dosage validation
- Background multithreaded reminder engine for medication schedules
- Medical history logging for diagnoses and lab reports
- Adherence reporting and CSV file export
